Ms. Ananya Sen is our senior clinical nutritionist, specializing in childhood eating behaviors, infant weaning, growth charts, and teen metabolism management. Supervised by Dr. Jyothirmayi, Ms. Ananya creates evidence-based, child-approved dietary regimes that ensure children receive full macro and micronutrient requirements during key development years.

Clinical Nutrition Philosophy

At Cherish Clinic, child nutrition is handled scientifically. We support Ellyn Satter's **Division of Responsibility in Feeding**:

• The Parent's Job: To decide what food is offered, when it is served, and where.
• The Child's Job: To decide whether they will eat, and how much they will consume.

By removing force-feeding and stress during meal times, children naturally learn to check internal hunger and fullness signals, preventing metabolic issues and picky eating triggers later in development.
