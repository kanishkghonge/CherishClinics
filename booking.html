Cherish Children’s Clinic was established on March 30, 2019, as a trusted pediatric clinic in Kondapur, Hyderabad. After serving children and parents through physical consultations, Cherish is now evolving into Cherish Health — a digital wellness platform created to support parents in India and across the world through online consultations, parenting guidance, digital courses and trusted child-health resources. Our goal is to help parents raise healthier, happier children with more confidence.
