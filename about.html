Dr. Jyothirmayi Kavipurapu

MD Pediatrics | Founder & Consultant Pediatrician

Dr. Jyothirmayi Kavipurapu is an MD Pediatrician and the Founder of Cherish Children's Clinic, Hyderabad. Her practice brings together clinical pediatrics, preventive care, child development awareness and practical parent guidance. She is known for communicating pediatric health concepts in a simple, reassuring and non fear-based manner, helping parents make informed choices without panic or confusion.

Her clinic work includes preventive pediatric consultations, parent counselling, lifestyle-focused guidance and building a broader wellness ecosystem for children and families. Her areas of interest include Preventive Pediatrics, Autism Spectrum Disorders, Pediatric Allergy, Parent Education, and child development and family guidance. Through Instagram @doc_mom_jyo, she shares practical, relatable pediatric education with clarity, empathy and responsible messaging.

Dr. Kalyan C. Battineni

MBBS, MD - Pediatrics | Associate Professor & Senior Consultant

Dr. Kalyan C. Battineni is a highly distinguished pediatrician and senior medical educator, bringing over a decade of comprehensive clinical experience to Cherish Children's Clinic. Along with his active practice, Dr. Kalyan serves as an Associate Professor of Pediatrics, contributing to medical literature and mentoring future child specialists.

Dr. Kalyan specializes in pediatric diagnostic systems, growth monitoring, and adolescent guidance. His dual role as an academic teacher and field practitioner keeps his approach strictly aligned with the latest clinical protocols and evidence-based medicine. Parents appreciate his detailed consultations, patient nature, and holistic approach to developmental health.
