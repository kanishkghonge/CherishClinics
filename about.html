Dr. Jyothirmayi Kavipurapu

MBBS, MD - Pediatrics | Consultant Pediatrician

Dr. Jyothirmayi Kavipurapu is a dedicated pediatrician, recognized as one of the best children's specialist doctors in Kondapur, Hyderabad. Popularly known online as "Doc Mom Jyo," she utilizes her digital platform to share expert parenting advice, nutrition checkups, and growth guidelines, supporting mothers and fathers in raising healthy kids.

Dr. Jyothirmayi adopts a conservative, evidence-based clinical philosophy. She is strongly committed to avoiding over-medication—prescribing heavy syrups, cough formulations, or antibiotics only when medically necessary. During consultations, she is extremely patient, listening to parents and making kids feel safe and comfortable to reduce clinical anxiety.
