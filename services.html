Specialized Pediatric Services

Providing expert clinical care from newborn monitoring through adolescent development schedules.

Newborn & Infant Care

Gentle support for your baby’s first weeks — from growth checks, cord care and feeding guidance to clearing common newborn doubts like crying, sleep and weight gain.

What to Expect
  • Well-baby checkups
  • Growth & weight monitoring
  • Breastfeeding and latching support
  • Cord care, jaundice & skin concerns
  • Crying, sleep & feeding guidance
  • Common newborn doubts answered

Routine Immunizations

Vaccinations as per national and IAP schedules, with gentle care, timely reminders and routine well-checks during your child’s vaccination visits.

What to Expect
  • Vaccine schedule planning
  • Timely reminders
  • Well-checks & growth review
  • Developmental assessment
  • Painless vaccine options
  • Post-vaccine care guidance

Fevers & Viral Infections

Quick assessment and practical guidance for common childhood fevers and viral illnesses — with clear advice on home care, medicine dosing, red flags and when to seek urgent care.

What to Expect
  • Fever assessment & illness care plan
  • Weight-based medicine dosing guidance
  • Tepid sponging advice for high fever
  • Febrile seizure safety & first-aid guidance
  • Red flags: when to reach hospital
  • Nearest emergency care guidance, if needed

Allergies & Asthma Care

Management support for childhood asthma, reactive airway symptoms, eczema flares, and food or environmental allergies.

What to Expect
  • Allergy trigger assessment
  • Eczema skin-care guidance
  • Inhaler/Nebuliser technique guidance
  • Asthma action plan & follow-up

Nutrition & Growth Support

Growth and nutrition guidance for feeding concerns, nutrient deficiencies, underweight or overweight children, and complementary feeding from 6 months onwards.

What to Expect
  • Growth percentile review
  • Nutrient deficiency assessment
  • Complementary feeding guidance
  • Underweight/overweight support
  • Picky eating strategies
  • Nutritionist counseling when needed

Developmental & Milestone Assessments

Screening for speech, motor, social, sensory and behavioral milestones — including autism and ADHD risk assessment, parent counseling, home strategies and therapy referral when needed.

What to Expect
  • Milestone tracking
  • Autism screening from 18 months
  • ADHD & behavior review
  • Speech, motor & sensory assessment
  • Parent counseling
  • Therapy referral, if needed

Comprehensive Wellness Checks

Complete wellness checkups for growing children — including physical examination, growth review, preventive health guidance and discussion of parent concerns.

What to Expect
  • Head-to-toe physical checkup
  • Growth & nutrition review
  • Age-appropriate health assessment
  • Preventive care guidance
  • Diet, sleep & lifestyle advice
  • Parent concerns addressed

Pre-Teen & Teen Wellness Checks

Pediatric wellness checks for pre-teens and teens, focused on growth, puberty, lifestyle, emotional health and long-term wellbeing — especially when routine doctor visits become less frequent after early childhood.

What to Expect
  • Growth, puberty & wellness review
  • Diet, sleep & activity guidance
  • Emotional health check-in
  • Understanding your teen better
  • Parent concerns addressed
  • Psychologist referral, if needed

Daycare Pediatric Care & Nebulisation

Same-day care for children needing supervised nebulisation, observation, IV fluids or doctor-advised injectable medications, with discharge by evening when appropriate.

What to Expect
  • Immediate nebulisation support
  • Nebulizer use & care guidance
  • Daycare observation
  • IV fluids, if needed
  • Injectable medicines, if advised
  • Hospital referral guidance
Cherish HEALTH Child • Teen • Parent Wellness

Cherish Children’s Clinic was established on March 30, 2019, as a trusted pediatric clinic in Kondapur, Hyderabad. After serving children and parents through physical consultations, Cherish is now evolving into Cherish Health — a digital wellness platform created to support parents in India and across the world through online consultations, parenting guidance, digital courses and trusted child-health resources. Our goal is to help parents raise healthier, happier children with more confidence.
