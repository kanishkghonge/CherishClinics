Specialized Pediatric Services

Providing expert clinical care from newborn monitoring through adolescent development schedules.

Newborn & Infant Care

Providing comprehensive support during your baby's first weeks of life, including growth curves monitoring, cord care, and maternal lactation counseling.

What to Expect
  • Well-baby checkups & health scans
  • Comprehensive weight & length mapping
  • Lactation and latching guidance
  • Post-natal feeding & sleep schedules advice

Routine Immunizations

Providing pediatric vaccination schedules conforming with both national and Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) recommendations, handled with a gentle touch.

What to Expect
  • Comprehensive vaccination calendar scheduling
  • Painless/painless vaccination variants options
  • Digital records & immunizations tracking
  • Post-vaccination side effects management guidance

Fevers & Viral Infections

Quick assessment and recovery management for standard childhood fever, coughs, gastric flu, vomiting, viral rashes, and acute infections.

What to Expect
  • In-clinic rapid temperature & hydration scans
  • Minimal medicine approach for viral recovery
  • Management of chest infections & bronchitis
  • Infectious disease recovery & home isolation advice

Allergies & Asthma Care

Management programs for pediatric respiratory asthma, reactive airway symptoms, chronic eczema rashes, and food or environmental allergies.

What to Expect
  • Allergy trigger assessment counseling
  • Eczema skin care routine customization
  • Asthma inhaler technique guidance for children
  • Long-term reactive airway treatment planning

Nutrition & Growth Support

Dietary planning for low weight indicators, vitamin deficiencies, child obesity guidance, and behavioral strategies for picky eaters.

What to Expect
  • WHO standard growth percentiles comparison
  • Customized micro-nutrient nutritional maps
  • Support strategies for stubborn picky eating habits
  • Pediatric weight management programs

Development Delays Screening

Clinical checking of speech milestones, sensory coordination, physical motor skills, early ADHD indicators, and developmental delays.

What to Expect
  • Interactive milestone tracking checkups
  • Speech, language, and hearing basic screens
  • Gross & fine motor skills assessments
  • Behavioral review & childhood ADHD guidelines counseling

Cherish Children's Clinic

Cherish Children’s Clinic, established on March 30, 2019, is a trusted pediatric clinic in Kondapur providing high-quality and compassionate care for children. Our goal is to support parents in raising healthy and happy children.
