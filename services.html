Online Pediatric Services

Expert online guidance for pregnancy readiness, newborn care, nutrition, development, parenting, and common child health concerns.

Pregnancy Preparation & Baby Readiness

Prepare for a confident start to parenthood with expert guidance before your baby arrives. Learn breastfeeding basics, comfortable feeding positions, birth planning, hospital bag essentials, baby shopping, and practical tips to help you feel ready for your newborn's arrival.

What to Expect
  • Breastfeeding preparation before delivery
  • Latch education and feeding positions
  • Breast care during pregnancy
  • Hospital bag checklist
  • Newborn shopping essentials
  • Setting up a safe home environment
  • What to expect in the first few days after birth
  • Preparing both parents for the transition

Newborn & First 100 Days

Support for your baby's most important early months, from feeding, weight gain and sleep to jaundice, skin care and common newborn concerns. Helping you build confidence through your baby's first 100 days.

What to Expect
  • Breastfeeding and formula feeding guidance
  • Latch assessment and feeding concerns
  • Weight gain and growth monitoring
  • Jaundice guidance
  • Cord care, skin care and newborn hygiene
  • Sleep, crying and soothing techniques
  • Reflux, colic and gas concerns
  • Routine newborn check-ups and when to seek medical care

Feeding & Nutrition

Personalized feeding guidance by a pediatrician and expert nutritionist, from breastfeeding and starting solids to healthy eating, growth, and nutrition at every stage of childhood.

What to Expect
  • Breastfeeding support
  • Formula feeding guidance
  • Starting solids
  • Picky eating
  • Growth & nutrition
  • Personalized diet plans by an expert nutritionist

Growth & Development

Expert online guidance to help you understand your child's growth, developmental milestones, behaviour, and learning at every stage.

What to Expect
  • Growth chart interpretation
  • Developmental milestone guidance
  • Autism & ADHD risk screening
  • Speech and motor development guidance
  • Behaviour and learning support
  • Therapy and specialist referral guidance

Vaccinations & Preventive Care

Expert online guidance on vaccination schedules, catch-up immunizations, travel vaccines, and preventive healthcare for children.

What to Expect
  • Personalized vaccination schedules
  • Catch-up vaccination planning
  • Vaccine counselling & FAQs
  • Travel vaccination guidance
  • Vaccination planning for children returning to India from abroad
  • Preventive health guidance

Common Childhood Illnesses

Expert online consultations for common childhood illnesses, with practical home care advice, treatment guidance, and clear recommendations on when an in-person visit or emergency care is needed.

What to Expect
  • Fever management
  • Cough and cold guidance
  • Vomiting and diarrhea
  • Rashes and skin concerns
  • Home care and medication guidance
  • When to visit a doctor or emergency room

Teen Health & Parenting Support

Helping parents navigate the challenges of growing children, from puberty and emotional wellbeing to behaviour, parenting concerns, and building healthy family relationships.

What to Expect
  • Puberty education for boys & girls
  • Teen physical and emotional wellbeing
  • Screen time & digital habits
  • Behaviour and parenting guidance
  • Sibling rivalry & parenting challenges
  • School, learning & emotional concerns

Parenting & Behaviour Support

Practical, evidence-based guidance to help parents navigate everyday parenting challenges and raise confident, emotionally healthy children.

What to Expect
  • Tantrums and challenging behaviours
  • Positive parenting strategies
  • Sleep and daily routines
  • Screen time guidance
  • Sibling rivalry
  • Emotional wellbeing and when to seek professional support

Postnatal Mom Wellness

Supporting mothers through the postpartum journey with expert guidance on physical recovery, emotional wellbeing, breastfeeding, and the transition to motherhood.

What to Expect
  • Postpartum recovery guidance
  • Breastfeeding challenges
  • Postnatal depression screening
  • Baby blues vs. postnatal depression
  • Maternal nutrition and self-care
  • Referral to mental health professionals when needed

Courses & Parent Education

Learn at your own pace with expert-designed courses, webinars, and practical resources to help you make confident parenting decisions.

What to Expect
  • Self-paced online courses
  • Live webinars and workshops
  • Parent education programs
  • Downloadable guides and resources
  • Expert Q&A sessions
  • Exclusive learning content
